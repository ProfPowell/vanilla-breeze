Returns policy

Marlowe Books accepts returns of new books in unread condition. Second-hand books are sold as seen.

Return a book within 14 30 days of purchase with the receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method, or are given as store credit if you paid in cash.

Books bought as gifts can be exchanged without a receipt until 15 January.

Orders placed online can be returned by post or to the shop on Quay Street.
