Word Break Opportunity

Long URLs: arbitrary break vs chosen break

VB's overflow-wrap: break-word keeps both URLs inside the box. Without <wbr> the line breaks wherever the edge falls; with it, the line breaks after a slash.

Without wbr: https://example.com/very/long/path/to/some/deeply/nested/resource/page.html

With wbr: https://example.com/very/long/path/to/some/deeply/nested/resource/page.html

Long compound words

Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious

Technical identifiers

AbstractSingletonProxyFactoryBean

Long URL in Japanese text

Breaks between kana, kanji and Latin words are already allowed. Put <wbr> inside the long Latin token.
