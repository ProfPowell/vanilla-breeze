Route 14 timetable changes

What is changing

From Monday 2 November, Route 14 between Harbour Square and Northfield runs every 10 minutes on weekdays instead of every 15. The first bus leaves Harbour Square at 05:40 and the last at 23:50.

Weekend buses run every 20 15 minutes, and the Sunday service now ends at 22:30 23:00.

Stops that move

The Mill Lane stop moves 80 metres north, to the new shelter outside the library. The Canal Street stop closes. Use Wharf Road, a three minute walk, or Mill Lane.

Step-free access

Both the new Mill Lane shelter and Wharf Road have raised kerbs. Every bus on the route has a ramp at the front door.

Tickets

Fares do not change. A ticket bought for the old timetable is valid on any Route 14 bus on the same day.
