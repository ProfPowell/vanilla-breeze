Booking a flu vaccination

Riverside Clinic gives flu vaccinations from October to February. Book online, by phone on 555 0142, or at the front desk. Appointments take about ten minutes.

Bring your patient card. If you are over 65, pregnant or have a long-term condition, the vaccination is free.
