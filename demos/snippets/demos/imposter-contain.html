data-layout-contain — the overlay never grows past its container (minus the margin) and scrolls inside instead.

Terms of service

This overlay holds more text than its 180px container can show. Because it is contained, it stays inside the container and scrolls.

Without data-layout-contain the card would grow past the container's edges.

The limit is the containing block: the positioned ancestor here, or the viewport when combined with data-layout-fixed.
