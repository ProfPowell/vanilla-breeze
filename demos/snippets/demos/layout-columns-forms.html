Layout Columns: element and attribute forms

Element form — <layout-columns>

The Measure

Typographers call the optimal line length the measure. Bringhurst places ideal prose at 45 to 75 characters; the original Penguin paperbacks ran to about 60.

When a line runs past 80 characters, the eye works harder to find its return. It sweeps back, hunting for the start of the next line, and sometimes lands on the wrong one.
