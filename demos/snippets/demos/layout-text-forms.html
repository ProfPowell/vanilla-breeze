Layout Text: element and attribute forms

Element form — <layout-text>

Reading Width

Measure and vertical rhythm on a wrapper element.

Rhythm

Headings get more space above than below, so they sit with the text they introduce.

Attribute form — <article data-layout="prose">

Reading Width

The same measure and rhythm on the article itself.

Rhythm

Headings get more space above than below, so they sit with the text they introduce.

Measure only — <article data-measure="narrow"> (centered, no rhythm)

Reading Width

No rhythm
