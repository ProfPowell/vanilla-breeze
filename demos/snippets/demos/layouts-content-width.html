Winter timetable

From the first Monday in November, buses on Lines 4 and 11 run every twelve minutes at peak times and every twenty minutes through the middle of the day. The last departures from Central Station move thirty minutes earlier on weekdays.

Line 27 to the airport is unchanged. Night services return on Fridays and Saturdays, and day tickets stay valid until the last bus of the night.

Fares

Single fares stay at their current price until the spring review. Children under eleven travel free with an adult.
