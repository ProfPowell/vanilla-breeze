Emphasis and Alternate Voice

Stress emphasis (em)

I love chocolate. (emphasis on "love")

I love chocolate. (emphasis on "chocolate" — different meaning)

Do not press that button.

Alternate voice (i)

The word persona is Latin for "mask."

She thought, this can't be happening, as the screen went black.

The USS Enterprise set sail at dawn.

The species Tyrannosaurus rex lived during the Cretaceous period.

Combined bold + italic

Bold italic via b+i — VB uses font-synthesis: none to prevent faux bold-italic.

Bold italic via strong+em — both semantic pairs work correctly.

Emphasis inside italic contexts (toggle)

The blockquote is already italic, so emphasis inside it switches back to upright.

The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy — emphasis inside a title flips the same way.

Never, and I mean never, press that button. — nested emphasis toggles too.

We sighted the Endurance at first light — the ship name is upright inside the italic quote.

Logbook of the Nimrod

Notes on Bellis perennis — a quoted title is upright, so <i> inside it stays italic.

CJK emphasis marks

これはとても大切です。

这是非常重要的。

CJK fonts have no italic face, so <em> uses emphasis marks instead: sesame dots over the text for Japanese and Korean, filled dots under the text for Chinese.

CJK alternate voice (i)

船の名前は日本丸です。

他心想：这不可能。

Romanised: Nihon Maru keeps its italic.

<i> in CJK text is upright in a calligraphic face with a dotted underline — not emphasis marks, which belong to <em>.
