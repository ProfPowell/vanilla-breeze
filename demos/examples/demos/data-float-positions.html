data-float — 8 standard positions

Each pill below uses data-float="…" to pin itself against the viewport. Resize the window to confirm safe-area insets and reflow.

The eight pills are pinned to the viewport edges, not to this text. On a right-to-left page left and right swap sides, while *-center stays on the viewport midpoint.

Several items in one corner? Wrap them in a <fab-stack> instead of floating each one — see the fab-stack demo.
