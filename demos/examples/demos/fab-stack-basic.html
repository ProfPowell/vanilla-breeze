Corner action stacks

Two <fab-stack> elements are pinned to the viewport. The one in the bottom corner holds a primary Compose button, a secondary Chat button, and a back-to-top link that appears once you scroll past the first screen.

The first child is always the primary action: it sits nearest the anchored edge and receives focus first. In the bottom stack that puts Compose at the bottom; in the top stack it puts Search at the top.

Scroll down to reveal the back-to-top link. Until it is visible it is also out of the tab order, so keyboard users never land on an invisible control.

Press Tab to walk the stacks and compare the focus order with the visual order.
