Recently Visited

Reads and writes localStorage only. This page is recorded because it contains a <recently-visited> element; a site records every page by adding <meta name="vb:track-history" content="on"> to its shared head. The page you are on is left out of the list. Use the Clear history button to wipe it, or press Pause tracking to stop recording new visits without losing existing entries.
