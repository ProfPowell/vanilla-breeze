On the Nature of Slow Reading

There is a particular quality of attention that long-form reading demands, a patience the eye must relearn each time we return to it.

The Measure

Typographers call the optimal line length the measure. Ideal prose sits at roughly 45 to 75 characters per line; beyond that the eye loses its place on the return sweep.

Pagination and Flow

The page, unlike the scroll, imposes a contract: a small act of completion at each turn, a breath between clauses.
